2. Test chatbot

3. Test overall credibility

curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/reports/1/chat -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"question":"What is the overall credibility score?"}'

curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/reports/1/chat -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"question":"Who is the author of this paper?"}'

If your history shows a different ID, replace 1 with that ID.
